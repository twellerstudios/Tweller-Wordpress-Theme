**URL parameters** (Ad → Tracking → URL parameters):

**Button:** Book Now · **Optimise for:** Leads (pixel `Lead`).

How Meta shows text: only the first ~125 characters of the primary text show before "…See more", so every variant puts the hook and the price/offer up front. Add **all** variants below to one ad ("Add text options") and Meta rotates them and learns which wins. Keep emojis to one or two.

## Testing plan (first week, $10/day)
- Run Ad 1 and Ad 2 together; hold Ad 3 back.
- Judge on **cost per Lead** after ~3 days (a Lead = a booking, a WhatsApp tap or a Call tap). Don't touch it for the first 48 hours.
- If one ad has clearly worse cost per Lead after 3 days, pause it and put Ad 3 in its place.
- Reply to every comment within the hour (use the quick replies above): it lifts delivery.
- Check the pixel in Events Manager → Test events once before launch.
