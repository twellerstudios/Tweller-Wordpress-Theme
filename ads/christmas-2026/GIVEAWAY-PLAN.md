# The Christmas Memory Giveaway: campaign plan

**The idea in one line:** *"Tell us the Christmas you'll never forget."* People comment their most memorable Christmas memory, tag the person who was there, and the memories that touch the most hearts win photo sessions.

**Why it spreads:**
- **It's emotional.** People share stories, not "me!" comments.
- **Every tag is a real person** from the memory ("@mom, remember the pastelles?"). That person gets notified, feels something, and comments their own memory. Each entry naturally pulls in one more person.
- **Likes decide the Full Session,** so entrants send their friends and family to like their comment. That's free reach for your post.
- **The website entry earns a bonus ticket** and a TT$50 thank-you code, so social traffic turns into site visits and bookings.

## 1. Prizes and how winners are picked

**Six winners: two per platform (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).**

| Prize | How it's won | On each platform |
|---|---|---|
| **Full Christmas Session** | The memory comment with the **most likes** when entries close ("Most-loved memory") | 1 |
| **Mini Session** | A **random draw** from everyone who commented a real memory (8+ words) | 1 |

- **Validity:** prizes can be used for **any session type within 6 months**.
- **Prize codes:** the plugin creates them. Each is 100% off, one-time, and expires after 6 months.
- **Draw tickets:** 1 for a memory comment, +1 for tagging the person who was there (Instagram and TikTok only), and +1 for also sharing the memory on the website.
- **Thank-you gift for every website entrant:** code `MEMORY50`, TT$50 off Christmas photos, valid until Dec 15.

> If you'd rather give away only one Full and one Mini in total, use the platform with the most entries for the Full, and draw the Mini from all three. The tool works either way.

## 2. Platform rules (so posts don't get flagged)
- **Facebook: don't ask people to tag friends or share to their timeline to enter.** That breaks Meta's Page promotion rules, so on Facebook the entry is just the memory comment. The tool never gives tag tickets on Facebook.
- **Instagram:** "tag the person in your memory" is fine because it's an accurate tag. Never ask people to tag people who aren't in the memory.
- **All three platforms:** every post needs the line *"This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Facebook/Instagram/TikTok."* It's already on the giveaway page.
- **No purchase necessary,** 18+, T&T residents only. The full rules are on the giveaway page.

- **2–8 s:** three quick memory lines appear over your before/after slider shots: *"The year the power went out and we sang by candlelight…"* · *"Mummy saved all year for one bicycle…"* · *"Grandma's pastelles, flour everywhere…"*

- **14–18 s:** "Tag the person who was there 🎄" + your logo. Keep text above the bottom 35% (the Reels safe zone).
- **Audio:** soft parang or piano Christmas audio from the platform's library, so it's licensed for the platform.

## 5. Keeping it going (the engagement loop)
- **Reply to every memory within a few hours** with something personal ("Pastelles AND parang?! 😭❤️"). Replies boost the post in the algorithm.
- **Memory of the Day** (stories): a quote card with a first name only, plus a "Comment yours on our post" link sticker.
- **Leaderboard story (Wed Nov 25 and Thu Dec 3):** "These 3 memories are leading 👀". Families mobilise and the likes snowball.
- **Story polls:** "Christmas morning: presents first or breakfast first?" Keeps people tapping between posts.
- **Countdown sticker** to Dec 6 on Instagram stories, so followers get a reminder when it ends.
- **Don't** like or reply in a way that favours entrants, and **don't** buy likes. Remove entries with obviously bought likes (the rules allow it).

## 6. Turning it into bookings
1. **The website entry** gives everyone the `MEMORY50` code (TT$50 off, until Dec 15) and sends them to the Christmas page.
2. **Snap It Forward:** anyone who books gets their own link to share.
3. **Retargeting:** in Ads Manager, make a custom audience of people who engaged with your Facebook/Instagram posts in the last 30 days, then run the Christmas image ad to them from Dec 8. Website entrants also fire a `GiveawayEntry` pixel event, which you can build an audience from.
4. **Tagged people:** **Giveaway → Everyone tagged** lists everyone your entrants tagged. These are warm leads; a friendly reply on their comment works better than a cold DM.
5. **Track it with UTM links:** use `twellerstudios.com/giveaway/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=memory-giveaway` in your bio and stories (`utm_source=facebook` / `tiktok` on the others).

## 7. Tracking and picking winners (in WordPress)
**Tweller Bookings → Giveaway**

1. **Get the comments.** After entries close, export each post's comments to CSV with a comment-export tool. ExportComments.com handles Facebook, Instagram and TikTok and includes usernames, likes and text. You can also paste lines as `username | likes | comment`.
2. **Import them** on the platform's tab. Re-importing is safe: duplicates are skipped and like counts are updated.
3. **The tab shows:**
   - comments, people, eligible entrants, draw tickets, total likes and tags;
   - everyone tagged, with who tagged them;
   - every entrant, with likes, tickets, tags and whether they also entered on the website.
4. **Click "Most-loved memory (Full Session)",** then **"Random draw (Mini Session)".** Each person can only win once. Every draw is logged with the number of eligible entrants and tickets and a reference code, so you can show it on the live stream.
5. **Click "Create Full/Mini Session code",** then **Copy**, and send it to the winner. If they don't reply within 72 h, click **"No reply: cancel win"** and draw again.
6. **Download all (CSV)** for your records.

**Results to check after the giveaway:**
- **Coupons → Coupon report:** how many bookings used `MEMORY50`.
- **Giveaway tab:** entries per platform.
- **Snap It Forward:** new friend bookings.
- **Meta Insights / Metricool:** reach, comments and saves on the giveaway posts.
